# Privacy Policy for Draft Dash

Effective date: 2026-06-06

Draft Dash is designed to work offline and store data locally on your device.

## Information We Collect

Draft Dash does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly from you.

The app may store the following locally on your device:

- League name
- Manager names and roster order
- Draft settings
- Draft history
- Theme and sound preferences

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

Draft Dash includes optional crash reporting via Sentry. If enabled in a build, the app may send crash reports and diagnostic information to help us investigate issues. This data does not include your league content unless it is included in a crash context.

## Local Storage

Your draft data is stored on-device using the platform’s local storage.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

- Sentry, for crash reporting if configured in the build
- Apple App Store / Google Play services required for app distribution

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher at the email address used for app store listings.
